# The Excuse That Almost Works

make.fun bounty entry. A one-line excuse a gamer texts the boss to skip work on GTA 6 launch week, at most 20 words.

The entry program writes the line to `entry.txt`:

> Council closed my street for resurfacing and my car's boxed in behind the steamroller till five

The excuse never names the game. A boring, specific, checkable detail (a closed street, a car trapped behind a steamroller) is exactly what a tired manager almost believes, and the joke lives in that logic: the slowest vehicle on earth is the reason you cannot get to work.
